# 📸 Content Upload Guide for GemFitness

## 🎬 Videos Needed

Upload training session videos to `/public/videos/`:

1. **hiit.mp4** - HIIT/Bootcamp class (30-60 sec)
2. **strength.mp4** - Strength training session (30-60 sec)
3. **cardio.mp4** - Cardio workout (30-60 sec)
4. **core.mp4** - Core/Ab workout (30-60 sec)
5. **boxing.mp4** - Boxing fitness class (30-60 sec)
6. **yoga.mp4** - Yoga/stretching session (30-60 sec)
7. **hero-video.mp4** - Hero section background (15-30 sec loop)

**Tips:**

- Keep videos under 10MB each for fast loading
- 1080p or 720p resolution
- Landscape orientation (16:9 ratio)
- Good lighting and audio
- Show energy and community!

## 🎨 Photo Guidelines

**Professional Quality:**

- Good lighting (natural or studio)
- Clear focus
- High resolution (at least 1080p)
- No heavy filters (keep it authentic)

**Content Ideas:**

- Members mid-workout (action shots)
- Group class energy
- Trainer demonstrations
- Equipment close-ups
- Facility wide shots
- Sunrise/sunset atmosphere shots

**Where to Get Content:**

1. Hire a local photographer for 1 day
2. Use high-quality phone camera (iPhone/Samsung)
3. Ask members to share workout photos (with permission!)
4. Record classes for video content
